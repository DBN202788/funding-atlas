Conservative fiscal policies produce real results. In keeping with the fiscal
responsibility that I have championed for the Free State of Florida during the past eight years,
I am proud to announce a fourth consecutive year ofa budget reduction.
The Fiscal Year 2026-27 Budget represents the culmination of eight years of
conservative leadership built on fiscal discipline, strategic investments, and a commitment to
delivering results for Floridians. This budget demonstrates that Florida can continue
investing in its priorities while maintaining one of the nation 's lowest per-capita state budgets.
With today's vetoes totaling $1.7 billion, including nearly $810 million in line-item
vetoes, our state reserves will be at a healthy $17.8 billion, up from just $11 billion in 2019.
Florida still maintains its AAA credit rating, we have - once again - maxed out the Rainy
Day Fund, and paid down over 50 percent of the state's historical debt.
During my tenure, the State has reduced more than 1,300 government positions while
serving a population that has grown by more than 10 percent, reinforcing the fact that we can
always do more with less.
Since 2019, now with the largest and most successful school choice program in the
country, Florida has invested over $30 billion into education, with historic per-student
funding reaching $9,338; we have invested $7.1 billion into teacher salary increases; and most
importantly, prioritized students' education over indoctrination.
The Florida Job Growth Grant Fund has supported more than 43,000 new jobs and
33,000 workforce education opportunities, while the Hometown Heroes Program has helped
thousands of Floridians achieve homeownership. To keep pace with Florida's growing
population, a strategic commitment of $63 billion over the next five years is targeted towards
congestion relief and infrastructure projects.
